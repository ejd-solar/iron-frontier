IRON FRONTIER

SKIRMISH SETUP
Touch: tap select · tap ground move · tap enemy attack · drag box-select · double-tap selects type · two fingers pan/pinch · Wall: tap it, then drag one finger to lay a line (tap = one block) · minimap jumps · 🛡 Guard button then tap area/friendly · sidebar tabs at top
PC: left-click select / move / attack · left-drag box select · hold right-drag pan (right-click cancels) · A attack-move · S stop · G guard (click area/friendly; +/− radius) · F1–F8 sidebar tabs · Ctrl+1-9 groups · wheel zoom · edge/arrow scroll · build hotkeys Q-P (P Repair Bay) · units: Flamer ; · Medic [ · Repair Drone ] · Engineer ' (others on buttons) · click damaged friendly with repairer = repair · click your Warp Gate = teleport · Space home · / Wall (drag a line) · \ Gate
Anti-air & airlift: Skyguard missile troopers (Barracks) and Hornet AA tanks (Vehicle Works) shred aircraft but are weak vs ground · Atlas Sky-Crane (Airfield) carries one tank or vehicle: select the Atlas and tap a vehicle (or select vehicles and tap the Atlas), then Unload — if it is shot down the vehicle drops damaged · Medium/High graphics use the detailed art
Expansion: Field Hub Carrier (Vehicle Works) drives anywhere and deploys into a new Command Hub — ⛺ Deploy button, D key, or double-tap it; the Atlas can airlift it · Own several Barracks / Vehicle Works / Airfields / Shipyards / Forges? Double-tap one (or ★ Set primary) and new units appear there
Walls & gates: Defence tab · Wall ($30) is a cheap, quick 1-tile block that stops ground units (aircraft fly over) and joins up with its neighbours — tap for one, drag for a line · Gate ($120, needs Power Plant) opens for your units, stays shut to enemies · enemies with no way in will smash through · keep gaps for harvesters · select a wall and tap ▲ to upgrade the whole connected line: Sandbags → Concrete ($25 a block) → Reinforced Steel ($45 a block)
Privacy: an optional anonymous play counter (no cookies, no personal data) may count launches and matches; open with ?notrack=1 or turn on Do Not Track to opt out
AI difficulty: harder AIs play smarter, not richer — they scout and counter your army, save up for bigger pushes, flank, pull back damaged units, guard their harvesters and expand. Only Brutal gets a small (+10%) income edge.
Economy: 🔷 crystal spires regrow nearby fields (rate set by Resources) · build a Crystal Extractor (X) beside a spire for +4/s · send an Engineer onto a neutral ⛽ derrick for +5/s (enemies can steal it) · each captured warp-gate pair gives +2/s · selling refunds 50%
